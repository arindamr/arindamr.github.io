Read the rows, not a single cell. The right question is not "which is cheapest" but "how long am I actually keeping this car", because the answer changes.

Which holding period is cheapest?

Total cost obviously rises the longer you keep the car, so comparing totals across periods is meaningless. The right measure is equivalent annual cost: discount each period's total back to today, then annuitise it. That is the standard way to compare projects of unequal life, and it answers the actual question — how much does a year of this car cost me, and does that get better or worse the longer I hold it?

Mileage cap — is it cheaper to take 8,000 and pay the excess?

Tesla's five real options, read off the configurator on 30 July 2026. At 0% APR every one totals £37,990 payable — raising the cap simply moves money out of the Optional Final Payment and into the monthly. So the cap is not really a price decision. It is a bet on whether you will settle the OFP and keep the car, or hand it back.
